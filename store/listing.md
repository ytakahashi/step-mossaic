## Description

Limit: 4,000 characters.

```
Step Mossaic turns your everyday steps into something calm to look at, not a scoreboard to keep up with.

Connect Apple Health, and your daily step counts become two quiet visuals: a mosaic-style heatmap of your walking rhythm, and a moss ball — a "marimo" — that grows over the course of each month.

HOW IT WORKS

Every day you walk, that day's total is added to your heatmap. Shading is relative to your own recent activity, not a fixed daily target, so there's no number to hit.

At the same time, this month's marimo is quietly taking shape:
- Its size reflects how much you walked this month.
- Its color reflects how active your walking was, relative to your usual pace.
- The texture of its edge reflects how even or uneven your walking was, day to day.

No single bad day changes much. A quiet week doesn't break anything — growth just slows, it never reverses. When the month ends, that marimo joins the shelf as a small, permanent record of how the month actually went, and a new one starts growing.

DESIGNED TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

- No step goals, streaks, or reminders. Nothing to lose, nothing pinging you to walk.
- No rankings or comparisons with other people. Everything is measured against your own recent pace.
- No decorative gamification. The heatmap and the marimo are the whole picture — see your rhythm at a glance, not a dashboard of stats.

WHAT'S INSIDE

- Home: today's steps, this month's growing marimo, and a heatmap you can view across different time ranges.
- Shelf: every past month's marimo, kept exactly as it looked when the month closed — a quiet timeline you can scroll back through.
- Settings: check your Health connection and rebuild the local cache if you ever need to.

PRIVACY, BY DESIGN

Step Mossaic only reads your step count from Apple Health — it never writes to Health, and it has no other access. Your daily totals and generated marimos are cached on your device so the app works offline; nothing is ever sent to a server, shared with third parties, or used for ads or analytics. There's no account, no sign-in, and no tracking of any kind.

Step Mossaic is built for iPhone, requires iOS 18 or later, and works entirely offline once your steps are synced.
```
